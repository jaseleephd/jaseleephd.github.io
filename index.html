Principal Scientist, Director at Salesforce

Building voice agents for Slack.

I lead research and applied AI work at Salesforce, where I focus on voice agents for Slack and practical large language model systems for real products. My work sits at the intersection of language, interfaces, and deployment: building models that are useful, reliable, and ready to ship.

Previously, I worked on autonomy at Tesla, strategy and technical direction at Cruise, and completed my PhD in machine learning at New York University under Kyunghyun Cho. My research background spans sequence modeling, non-autoregressive generation, dialogue, and grounded language learning.
